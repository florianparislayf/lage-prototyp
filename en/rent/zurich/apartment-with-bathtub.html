How Lage checks “Bathtub”

AI looks at the listing photos and the floor plan for a bathtub, not just a shower, and reads the text as well. It also notes whether the bathroom has a window, which brings in daylight and helps against damp.

More on the method

What to check

  • On a floor plan, a bathtub is usually drawn as a long rectangle, while a shower is usually a square.
  • If the bathroom has no window, ask how it is ventilated, since poor ventilation can lead to mould.
  • In older buildings, ask about the size of the hot water boiler, which may not fill a tub twice in a row.

Questions

Why not just use the number of bathrooms?

The number of bathrooms does not say whether there is a tub or only a shower. Lage looks at the photos and the floor plan instead.

Does a shower over the bath count?

Yes. A bathtub with a shower over it counts as a bathtub.

Why does a bathroom window matter?

A window lets moisture out and daylight in. Lage shows it as a separate detail, based on the photos and the floor plan.
