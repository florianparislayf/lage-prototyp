How Lage checks “Near Zurich HB”

For each address, Lage calculates the trip to Zurich HB with the public transport timetable and the footpath network, including the walk to the stop. Only apartments that are 15 minutes or less away, door to door, are included.

More on the method

What to check

  • Ask which side the bedrooms face, as streets and rail lines near the station can be loud at night.
  • Try the trip yourself at the time you would usually leave for work.
  • Also consider areas with a direct S-Bahn connection, where a few stops by train can be quicker than a tram ride across the city.

Questions

How does Lage define “near the main station”?

Up to 15 minutes door to door to Zurich HB, on foot or by public transport, calculated from the timetable. Being in the same district or postcode is not enough.

Does the travel time include walking and changes?

Yes. It runs from the front door to the station, including the walk to the stop and any change on the way.

Is an apartment near the station noisy?

Not necessarily. Lage also shows road and rail noise at night at the building’s facade, from the federal noise maps.
