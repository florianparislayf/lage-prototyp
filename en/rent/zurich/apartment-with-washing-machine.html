How Lage checks “Own washing machine”

AI reads the listing text and looks at the photos for a washing machine, a dryer or a stacked washer-dryer inside the apartment. A shared laundry room does not count, even if it has new machines.

More on the method

What to check

  • Ask whether the machine belongs to the apartment or to the outgoing tenant, who may want to sell it to you.
  • Check the house rules, as quiet hours at night and on Sundays often apply to your own machine too.
  • If you want to install a machine yourself, ask the landlord first, since it needs water and drain connections.

Questions

What is a Waschküche?

It is the shared laundry room that many Swiss apartment buildings have, usually in the basement. Tenants book it on a schedule (Waschplan), which is why a machine of your own is in demand.

Does a washing tower count?

Yes. A washing tower (Waschturm) is a washing machine with a dryer stacked on top, and Lage counts it as your own machine.

Who pays for repairs to the machine?

If the machine belongs to the apartment, repairs are generally the landlord’s responsibility, while small upkeep such as cleaning the filter is up to you. Check your lease for the details.
